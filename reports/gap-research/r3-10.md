## 2. Firming adder: €15 is too low

A flat-load buyer of a pay-as-produced PPA buys power on the market when generation is short and sells the surplus when it is long. The cost per MWh of load is:

**Firmed price = PPA price + B × (1 − capture rate) × (1 − share of the profile the flexible potline absorbs) + risk premium + fee**

| Tranche | PPA price | Profile cost | Risk + fee | **Firmed** | **Firming adder** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wind, no flex | 66 | 67×0.18 = 12.1 | 5.5 | **83.6** | **17.6** |
| Solar, no flex | 45 | 67×0.45 = 30.2 | 5.5 | **80.7** | **35.7** |
| 70/30 wind/solar hybrid, no flex (capture rate 0.739) | 59.7 | 17.5 | 5.5 | **82.7** | **23.0** (range 17–30) |
| Wind + ±25% flex (absorbs 35% of profile) [est.] | 66 | 7.9 | 5.5 | **79.4** | 13.4 |
| Solar + flex (absorbs 50% of profile) [est.] | 45 | 15.1 | 5.5 | **65.6** | 20.6 |
| Utility tailored 10-yr baseload | — | — | B + 4 | **71.0** | — |
| 10% equity in onshore wind, at cost + flex | 58 | 7.9 | 5.5 | **71.4** | — |

**Findings:**

1. **The adder is wrong for an unflexed hybrid.** The memo's €15/MWh adder applies only to wind-heavy supply that the potline flexes. For a hybrid without flex the correct figure is **€23/MWh (range €17–30)**.
2. **Firmed renewable baseload in Germany costs more than the forward curve.** At €80–84 it sits **€13–17 above B**. Current PPA prices (€66 wind) are above the expected capture value of wind output (0.82 × 67 = €55).

- **Cost per tonne:** €69 × 14.5 = **≈ €1,000/t Al**.
- **Gap to the memo's target:** the memo targets ≤€50–55/MWh. The gap is **€14–19/MWh, or €200–275/t**. Market PPAs in Germany cannot close it.

## 5. The ICC lapse cannot be offset by PPAs

- **Size of the ICC.** German ICC aid is calculated as 75% × 0.72 tCO2/MWh (the Central West Europe emission factor) × EUA price × the aluminium consumption benchmark of about 14.3 MWh/t. At an EUA price of €75 that is €40.5/MWh, or **≈ €575/t, ≈ €115m/yr per 200 kt** [est.].
- **Source and caveat.** Guidelines: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:52020XC0925(01). The benchmark value needs checking against the guidelines' annex.
- **ICC is not tied to the supply contract.** It is paid on the benchmark regardless of where the power comes from. A PPA only hedges the CO2 component of the power price.
- **Scale of the mismatch.** Even in the stress case the platform covers less than half of an ICC lapse, and in the base case close to none.
- **Real levers against an ICC lapse:**
  - extending the ETS state-aid guidelines beyond 2030;
  - extending CISAF / the German Industriestrompreis (€50/MWh on 50% of consumption, currently 2026–28; [PwC](https://legal.pwc.de/en/news/articles/green-light-for-electricity-price-relief-for-energy-intensive-users));
  - keeping the §19 StromNEV band-load grid-fee reduction, or replacing it with a flexibility-based fee. Losing it could cost about €8–12/MWh [est., verify the BNetzA reform];
  - adding indirect emissions to CBAM.
