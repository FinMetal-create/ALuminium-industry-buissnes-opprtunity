## 3. Gaps and caveats the CSO should see

- **IEA Critical Minerals Outlook does not model aluminium.** Its 2024 and 2025 editions cover copper, lithium, nickel, cobalt, graphite and rare earths. The only IEA aluminium figure is in the grids report (row 9).
- **Gigacasting and defence have no published tonnage.** Both rows are labelled proxies. The defence estimate is highly sensitive to the value share. ±50% is a fair range.
- **The CRU 2030 figure dates from 2022.** CRU's 2025 long-term outlook is paywalled. The 119.5 Mt is the latest public CRU number.
- **The Q1 2026 scrap export figure conflicts between sources:** 328 kt in one report, 346 kt in another. Use the 2025 annual 1.27 Mt.
- **Recycling capacity at risk:** about 15% of EU recycling capacity is idle. Constellium says its €130m Neuf-Brisach recycling expansion depends on scrap availability (https://www.alcircle.com/news/al-circle-analysis-europes-aluminium-scrap-curbs-indias-risk-and-uss-supply-opportunity-121410).

## 4. So what for our company

1. **The scrap-capture business case holds up.** 1.27 Mt/yr leaves the EU, roughly 29% of the EU's 4.4 Mt recycled output. Even with the export duty withdrawn, about 110 kt/yr of new beverage-can scrap must be collected under the PPWR deposit rules by 2029. Capturing 10% of the exported scrap would be **~127 kt/yr**, enough to run one large remelt-and-sort line.
2. **Primary metal is scarce, so the premium case is sound.** At 1.2 Mt of output against ~13.5 Mt of demand, and with CBAM in force, every tonne of EU-made low-carbon primary is structurally short. This supports a smelter restart or uprating investment, but it should be modelled on 1.2 Mt, not 2 Mt.
3. **Auto castings, about 1.7 Mt by 2030, is the largest product-form market we can reach.** Gigacasting is only ~150 kt of it [EST]. Position the investment as a high-recycled-content casting alloy for structural castings, with gigacasting as upside rather than the core case.
4. **Grids (0.8–1.3 Mt [EST]) and defence (60–150 kt [EST]) should go to the board as ranges.** Use the EC's €584bn and the EDA's €88bn as the hard anchors.
