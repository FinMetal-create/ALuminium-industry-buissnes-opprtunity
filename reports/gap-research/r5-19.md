## 2. A correction to the CEO's premise
EU **initiation notices** for expiry reviews normally describe import trends in words ("imports remained at significant volumes") and give no tonnage. *[Analyst judgement from EU trade-defence practice, not verified for these two notices.]* The tonnes show up in two places:
- **The non-confidential request on TRON.** These requests usually carry Eurostat-based volumes, sometimes as ranges or indices.
- **The definitive review regulation.** The review investigation period (RIP) is likely CY2025 for extrusions (initiated 27 Mar 2026) and about Jul 2025–Jun 2026 for flat-rolled (initiated about 9 Oct 2026). These are my assumptions about the usual RIP conventions. Findings would then come out around Q2 2027 and Q4 2027 to Q1 2028.

**Cross-checks.**
1. **The 7604 anchor.** Non-alloy profiles (2,253 t) are typically 5–10% of China's 7604 flow to the EU (my assumption). That puts 7604 at 23–45 kt and implies $2,850–5,700/t, which brackets the 27 kt central. On the central figure, China supplies about 7% of the 358 kt alloy-profile import pool and is down about 91% from the 2019 extrusions level. The 2019 base includes 7608 tubes, so the true fall is slightly smaller.
2. **Share of China's exports.**
   - 7606: 111 kt is 3.6% of China's 3.07 Mt plate/sheet/strip exports. Before the duties it was about 6–8%.
   - 7607: 67 kt is 5.0% of China's 1.34 Mt foil exports.
   - Both shares are consistent with duty-suppressed flows.
3. **Market share.**
   - The in-scope flat-rolled market is about 2.1 Mt, implied by 171 kt at an 8% share.
   - If 60–80% of 2025 Chinese 7606 is in scope (can stock, automotive and aerospace are excluded from the duty), the in-scope volume is 67–89 kt. That is a **3–4% share**, against 8–12% before the duties.
   - **These are my calculations.**

**Confidence.**
- Medium on 7604 (one hard sub-line supports it).
- Low-to-medium on 7606 and 7607. A ±15% unit-value error moves the total by ±35 kt.
- Low on 7601.
