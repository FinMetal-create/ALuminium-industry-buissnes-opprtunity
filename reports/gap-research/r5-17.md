## 3. Assumptions behind the re-run

The cost basis is set so it matches the v1 memo (`/home/user/ALuminium-industry-buissnes-opprtunity/reports/ceo-strategy-memo-v1-initial.md`, lines 39 and 316):
- **Specific consumption:** 14.0 MWh/t. This reproduces the memo's €2,000/t at €50 and €2,560/t at €90. The memo's 14.5 MWh/t gives the 2.9 TWh figure; 14.0 MWh/t gives 2.8 TWh/yr for 200 kt.
- **Non-power cash cost:** €1,300/t, which is €2,000 − 14 × 50.
- **Normalised revenue:** €2,370/t (memo line 316).
- **Delivered adder on top of the forward:** €6/MWh, range €3–12 [EST]. This covers reduced grid fees under §19 StromNEV, levies, and balancing and shaping for a flat load.
- **ICC (German Strompreiskompensation):** 75% aid intensity × 0.72 tCO2/MWh × EUA price. At an assumed EUA of €80/t [EST], that is about **€43/MWh**. **The current EU guidelines run to 2030.** So the central case for 2031 is **no ICC**, unless a successor scheme is adopted.

**Formulas:**
- Cash cost = 1,300 + 14 × (forward + 6)
- Margin = 2,370 − cash cost
- Per 200 kt: €/t × 0.2 = €m/yr

## 6. Firming build (2031 benchmark = Cal-31 + €6 = about €75/MWh delivered)

The test is whether a self-built firmed portfolio comes in below about €75/MWh.

| Component [EST] | €/MWh |
|---|---|
| DE onshore wind and solar hybrid PPA, pay-as-produced | 55–65 |
| Shape and profile cost to convert to baseload | 12–20 |
| Less potline flexibility of ±25% for 48 h (Trimet/EnPot), which takes on about 30–40% of the shape cost | −4 to −8 |
| Balancing and residual market top-up | 3–6 |
| **Firmed cost** | **66–83 (central about 74)** |

**At the central case the firming build saves only about €1/MWh, which is about €3m/yr per 200 kt.** In other words, it roughly matches the forward and does not beat it on price.

It is worth doing for two other reasons:
- **It fixes the price for 10–15 years.** That removes the ±€8 forward band, which is worth about ±€22m/yr.
- **It is more valuable if forwards land at the high end.** At €77, firming saves about €2.5m/yr per €1/MWh of excess over firmed cost.

**Decision rule for the paper:** sign hybrid PPAs only where firmed cost is at or below the Cal-31 settlement plus €6, minus €3. The €3 is a margin of safety. Below that, keep the exposure on the forward and hedge on EEX.
