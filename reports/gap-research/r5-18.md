## 4. Value at stake (my calculations; inputs from the earlier memo)

**Gross value under Branch A.** Duties are certain to stay in force for practically all of 2027, because the review runs 12–15 months and cannot finish before about November 2027. The renewal decision only affects 2028 onwards.

**Reconstructing the memo's €0.6–2.6m per 10 kt** (my reconstruction, not a sourced figure):
- Exposed revenue: 10 kt × about €4,000–4,500/t CIF China foil.
- Duty wedge: a weighted duty of about 10% gives about €400–450/t. The definitive rates are roughly 6–29% from memory; not verified.
- Pass-through to EU prices of about 15–60% gives €60–260/t, which is **€0.6–2.6m per 10 kt**.

**Expected value of 2027 protection today:** 0.80 × €0.6–2.6m = **€0.5–2.1m per 10 kt** (75–85% range: €0.45–2.2m).

**Under Branch C:**
- The 2027 value drops to about zero, apart from residual price stickiness. Assuming 10–20% of the wedge holds for 3–6 months gives roughly €0.03–0.13m per 10 kt.
- The expiry-review route is already closed. The only remedy would be a fresh complaint under Art. 5. That means 13–14 months to definitive duties, so no protection before about Q1 2028, and a cost I estimate at €0.5–1.5m.

**Dossier spend of €0.3–0.6m:**
- Under A with European Aluminium as applicant, our cost is mainly:
  - the Union-producer sampling reply, due about 7–15 days after initiation;
  - a full questionnaire if we are sampled;
  - a verification visit.
- I estimate that at €0.1–0.3m, plus €0.2–0.3m only if we want our own counsel driving the injury case.
- Under C, none of it is needed.

**Expected saving from waiting about 3 weeks:**
- Spend avoided under C: 0.2 × €0.3–0.6m = €0.06–0.12m.
- Under A, the cost of waiting is small, because the sampling deadline only starts at initiation.
