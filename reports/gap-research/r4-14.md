**Two corrections to the task's fallback plan:**

- **OJ C/2026/499 is not an initiation notice.** It is the January 2026 *notice of impending expiry* for the flat-rolled duties under Reg. 2021/1784 (expiry 12.10.2026) ([EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2026/499/oj); [HK TID](https://www.tid.gov.hk/en/tradecircular/2026/ci962026.html)). Notices of this kind contain no import volumes. The flat-rolled expiry review itself was opened on about 9 Oct 2026, after a June request from European Aluminium ([MLex](https://www.mlex.com/mlex/articles/2536071)). I have not seen its notice number.
- **OJ C/2026/1920 is unlikely to contain tonnes.** It opened the extrusions review on 27 Mar 2026 ([HK TID](https://www.tid.gov.hk/en/tradecircular/2026/ci2842026.html); [AL Circle](https://www.alcircle.com/news/ec-launches-expiry-review-of-ad-measures-on-chinese-aluminium-extrusions-117902)). Expiry-review initiation notices under Art. 11(2) normally only summarise the request's claims. Tonnes appear later, in the review regulation (extrusions about Mar–Jun 2027) or in the non-confidential version of the request on TRON.
- **Reg. 2025/1720 tonnage was not retrievable.** It covers foil in rolls (household foil), with a review investigation period of CY2023 ([EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2025/1720/oj/eng); [HK TID](https://www.tid.gov.hk/en/tradecircular/2025/ci6852025.html)). **Scope bias.** All of HS 7606 in 2025 is compared against a 2019 base that covers only the duty scope. The duty scope excludes can stock and aerospace products. As a result, the flat-rolled fall above is **understated**, and −39% is a floor.
