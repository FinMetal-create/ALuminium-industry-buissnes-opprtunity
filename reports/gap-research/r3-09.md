## 2. Will the converter-foil duties lapse on 9 Dec 2026?

**I cannot confirm this either way today.**

**The legal rule (high confidence).** Under Art. 11(2) of the Basic Anti-Dumping Regulation (EU) 2016/1036, measures expire 5 years after they are imposed. They stay in force if a notice of initiation of an expiry review is published in OJ C before that date. Duties then continue while the review runs, which can take up to 15 months, so into early 2028.

- The EU industry had to lodge its request no later than 3 months before expiry, which is about 9 Sep 2026.
- The Commission often publishes the initiation notice weeks after the request deadline, and sometimes on or just before the expiry date. A notice that has not appeared by 21 Oct would therefore be normal.
- If no notice appears by the expiry date, the Commission publishes a "notice of expiry" in OJ C.

**Dates (from memory, verify):**
- **Reg. 2021/2170 (converter foil):** published in OJ L 438 on 8 Dec 2021 and in force from 9 Dec 2021. It therefore expires at the end of 9 Dec 2026 unless a review is initiated. This matches the memo's date.
- **Reg. 2021/2287 (aluminium foil in rolls over 10 kg, 0.008–0.018 mm, the "jumbo household foil" expiry-review continuation):** this has a different date, probably late Dec 2026 (verify). The memo should not tie both measures to 9 Dec.

**Probability (my estimate, not sourced).** I put the chance that a review is initiated before 9 Dec 2026 at about 80–90%. The reasoning:

- EU expiry requests from an active complainant are initiated in the large majority of cases.
- The main counter-force is lobbying by downstream converters and flexible-packaging users.

## 3. Comext query specification

These are the parameters that replace the value-derived figures.

**Base query:**
- Dataset DS-045409, `freq=M`, `reporter=EU27_2020`, `partner=CN`, `flow=1` (imports), `indicators=QUANTITY_IN_100KG`.
- Divide the result by 10 to get tonnes, then sum the months to get each year and Jan–Aug.

**Example URL:**
`https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/comext/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/DS-045409?format=JSON&freq=M&reporter=EU27_2020&partner=CN&product=76071119&flow=1&indicators=QUANTITY_IN_100KG&sinceTimePeriod=2019-01`
