## Scrap-Tolerant, High-Recycled-Content & Crossover Alloys

*Research note: WebFetch and direct HTTP were blocked in this environment (DNS and egress refusals for amag-al4u.com, fastmarkets.com, sms-group.com, alcircle.com, arxiv.org and patents sites). Every figure below comes from search-engine extracts of the cited pages. Figures marked [est.] are my own reasoned estimates, and their basis is stated.*

### 1. Why this matters now

Europe is long on post-consumer scrap but short on alloys that can absorb it. European Aluminium reports that the EU exported a **record 1.27 Mt of aluminium scrap in 2025**, about 50% above 2019, mostly to Asia with India the largest buyer. Exports rose a further ~3% year to date to April 2026 ([engineeringnews](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/eu-aluminium-scrap-export-curbs-delayed-until-september-group-and-sources-say-2026-06-26)). Brussels has floated export curbs, including a reported **15% levy**, and has delayed them repeatedly ([alcircle](https://www.alcircle.com/news/eu-proposes-15-tax-on-aluminium-scrap-exports-to-secure-recycled-metal-supply-120114)). Stricter Waste Shipment Regulation rules apply from 2027 ([Recycling Europe](https://recyclingeurope.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/UPDATED-Recycling-Europe-feedback-CfE-aluminium-measures-16-Februrary-2026.pdf)).

The core technical problem is well documented. Scrap carries Fe, Si, Cu, Zn, Mn and Cr, which build up with each recycling loop. Today's wrought specifications have tight impurity windows, so producers dilute scrap with primary metal or downcycle it to castings. The 20-author review by Raabe, Pogatscher et al. ("the science of 'dirty' alloys", *Prog. Mater. Sci.* 128, 2022, built on about 1,300 papers) is the reference. It also notes that scrap supply is expected to roughly double by 2050 ([TU Wien repository PDF](https://repositum.tuwien.at/bitstream/20.500.12708/158251/1/Raabe-2022-Progress%20in%20Materials%20Science-vor.pdf)).

Standards bodies are starting to move. CEN/BSI revision notices for EN 1706 and EN 1676 state that "efforts are underway to incorporate recyclable alloys or to increase the allowable impurity levels… (see EN 573)". They link this to the expected ELV Regulation and future minimum recycled-content rules for vehicles ([BSI EN 1706 project](https://standardsdevelopment.bsigroup.com/projects/9026-13536)). The specification barrier is therefore beginning to loosen. Companies that bring qualified chemistries to the committees will shape the new windows.

### 2. Technical levers for impurity tolerance

| Lever | Mechanism | Evidence | Maturity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Widened Fe/Mn/Cr windows in 6xxx** | "Effective Si" governs strength. Mn and Cr turn needle-like β-AlFeSi into α-Al(FeMn)Si | Raising Fe from 0.2 to 0.7 wt% in AlMgSi: 6082 tolerates extra Fe better than 6005A, which tolerates it better than 6060 ([Met. Mat. Trans. A 2023 via AIT](https://publications.ait.ac.at/en/publications/tolerance-of-almgsi-wrought-alloys-for-high-fe-contents-the-role-/)). A Kaiser patent application claims a 6xxx alloy allowing **0.8% Fe and 0.8% Mn** with **>75–90% scrap** ([Justia US20230416879](https://patents.justia.com/patent/20230416879); [Mining Technology](https://www.mining-technology.com/data-insights/kaiser-aluminum-files-patent-for-eco-friendly-6xxx-series-aluminum-alloy-composition/)) | Industrial (extrusion) |
| **HRC 5xxx/6xxx body sheet** | Contaminant-tolerant variants of baseline automotive chemistries | 2025 TMS abstract: four HRC alloys tolerant of ELV contaminants. The 5xxx HRC alloys resist intergranular corrosion, but bendability drops ([BSI/TMS context](https://programmaster.org/PM/PM.nsf/ApprovedAbstracts/AC356C1C366B1DCC85258B4D00514B5F)) | Pilot / early industrial |
| **Crossover AlMgZn(Cu) ("5/7 crossover")** | Mg-rich 5xxx base with Zn(Cu) T-phase hardening, more tolerant of tramp elements | Leoben and AMAG Light Metals 2024 paper: crossover alloys "may also tolerate a higher content of impurity elements" ([Springer](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-50308-5_28)). Preprint on Fe/Si ratio and cooling-rate effects ([Leoben](https://pureadmin.unileoben.ac.at/portal/en/publications/influence-of-solidification-rate-and-impurity-content-on-57crossover-alloys(dd49947e-59a5-41e3-9941-17517c095140).html)) | Commercial launch, prototype auto parts |
| **Rapid solidification** (twin-roll / direct strip casting, AM) | Fine microstructure keeps Fe in solid solution and avoids coarse IMCs | Strip casting "significantly enhances Fe-tolerance" ([J. Sust. Metall. 2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40831-024-00844-z)). Roll casting can **double the Fe capacity of 6063** above the 0.35% JIS limit ([search extract](https://www.totalmateria.com/articles/twin-roll-casting-of-al-alloys-1/)). Deakin TMS 2026: Al-2.5Fe with most Fe in solid solution, and Fe speeds ageing of Al-1.5Cu-1.5Fe by about 10× ([TMS](https://www.programmaster.org/PM/PM.nsf/ApprovedAbstracts/B9DAFE308336844485258CB9001A0A4B)) | Lab to pilot |
| **Al-Ca-X "contamination-tolerant" alloys** | Fe and Mn do not degrade, and can improve, Al-Ca eutectic alloys | DFG project IDeAS (no. 562154592, since 2025) combines inverse design, alloy simplification and AM ([DFG GEPRIS](https://gepris.dfg.de/gepris/projekt/562154592)) | Research |
| **Melt purification** | Remove impurities rather than tolerate them | Alcoa ASTRAEA claims P0101 purity from zorba. Still R&D as of the last public statement ([Recycling Today](https://recyclingtoday.com/news/alcoa-zorba-aluminum-shredded-scrap-astraea-patent-purity); [S&P Platts](https://www.spglobal.com/platts/en/market-insights/latest-news/metals/120121-alcoa-weighing-future-role-in-secondary-aluminum-market-with-astraea-executive)) | R&D |

Fe-intermetallic modification with Mn, Cr, Sr and Ca, and the effect of Fe intermetallics on defects, strength and corrosion, is reviewed in Kotadia et al., *Materials Today Sustainability* 2025 (DOI 10.1016/j.mtsust.2025.101119) ([LJMU repository](https://researchonline.ljmu.ac.uk/id/eprint/26261/)).

### 3. Named players and products

- **AMAG CrossAlloy®.57 (Ranshofen, with Montanuniversität Leoben, Pogatscher group).** This is the first commercial 5/7 crossover sheet. It reaches Rp0.2 of **260–310 MPa in T4-FH and 350–400 MPa after paint bake (T64)**, against about 120 MPa for 6016-T4 and 130 MPa for 5182-O. AMAG reports adhesive bond strength about 20% above 6016 and 5182 ([AMAG automotive](https://www.amag-al4u.com/produkte/crossalloy/automotive)). The alloy won the **ALUMINIUM Innovation Award 2026** (Technology & Product Innovation) ([alcircle](https://www.alcircle.com/news/new-aluminium-alloy-from-ranshofen-wins-the-aluminium-innovation-award-2026-121566)). AMAG makes only qualitative recycling claims ("wider range of scrap streams") and publishes no percentage. Automotive parts are at prototype stage. From early Leoben research (about 2017–18 [est.]) to commercial launch took about **7–9 years [est.]**.
- **Novelis.** Average recycled content was **63% in FY2025**, with automotive alloys up to **85%** and a target of 75% by 2030. Advanz 6F-e170RC exterior sheet contains ≥75% recycled metal. A 100% ELV-scrap outer-panel sheet, still a prototype, won the 2026 MMK Award ([Novelis IR](https://investors.novelis.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1415/novelis-wins-award-for-100-recycled-end-of-life-vehicle-scrap-product)). It was runner-up for the 2026 Innovation Award. Novelis and The Future is NEUTRAL (Renault) target **>95% recycled, >50% ELV** skin sheet ([Novelis](https://novelis.com/news/novelis-and-the-future-is-neutral-drive-automotive-circularity-with-new-recycling-partnership/)). Earlier products are RC5754 (JLR, 2016, up to 75% recycled) ([Novelis IR](https://investors.novelis.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/190/novelis-works-with-jaguar-land-rover-to-create-rc5754-high-recycled-content-aluminum-alloy-for-automotive-industry)) and HRC57S (EN AW-5005 with **>90% recycled**) ([datasheet](https://novelis.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Novelis_HRC57S®_Product-Data-Sheet_03.2022_EN.pdf)). Novelis is also promoting **Uni-alloy™** car-body concepts ([S&P AutoTechInsight](https://autotechinsight.ihsmarkit.com/news/5278461/from-scrap-to-strength-novelis-europe-on-vehicle-lightweighting-and-sustainable-solutions)).
- **Hydro CIRCAL.** CIRCAL extrusion ingot contains ≥75% post-consumer scrap with an EPD of **1.9 kg CO2e/kg**. CIRCAL 100R is 100% post-consumer, below 0.5 kg CO2e/kg, and sold only in small batches ([Hydro](https://hydro.com/en-GB/aluminium/products/low-carbon-and-recycled-aluminium/recycled-aluminium/hydro-circal)). In September 2025 Hydro extended CIRCAL to **foundry alloys** (wheels, battery boxes, structural parts), certified at Alumetal Nowa Sól ([Light Metal Age](https://www.lightmetalage.com/news/industry-news/casthouse/hydro-expands-hydro-circal-product-offerings-to-include-foundry-alloys/)).
- **Constellium.** The CirConAl project builds on the HSA6® platform. Constellium showed crash-management systems made from **>90% post-consumer scrap** at Cenex 2025 ([Constellium](https://constellium.com/news/showcase-aluminum-solutions-collaborative-research-project-circonal-cenex2025)). Its €130m Neuf-Brisach recycling centre adds 130 kt/yr. Recycled input was 47% in 2025, with a target of ≥50% by 2030.
- **Speira.** Speira is rolling out SMS group's **Metallics Optimizer**, an AI charge-mix tool, at Töging, Grevenbroich and Deizisau, with an option for six more EU sites. It is also adding furnaces for low-grade and contaminated scrap ([Foundry Planet](https://www.foundry-planet.com/d/digital-solution-enhances-efficiency-reduces-costs-and-promotes-sustainable-circular-economy/)).
- **Others.** Kaiser holds the high-Fe/Mn 6xxx patent. Apple uses a custom 100% recycled enclosure alloy, UL-verified since 2018 ([Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2018/10/31/apple-designs-greenest-mac-ever/)). Rheinfelden is moving post-consumer scrap into primary casting alloys ([EUROGUSS profile](https://euroguss-mexico.german-pavilion.com/en/sites/exhibitors/127778)). ITU and ASAS reported high-recycled 6063, 6082 and CRM-lean 6111 that meet automotive requirements using MULTI-PICK sorted scrap (*Materials*, Jan 2026) ([PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12842945/)).

### 4. AI in alloy design and casthouse blending

- **Alloy rationalisation by ML.** Tiwari, Mendis and Eskin (*J. Sustain. Metall.* 2025) applied K-means and PCA to 292 datasets and reduced **42 grades of 6xxx to 10 "optimal alloys"** ([Brunel](https://bura.brunel.ac.uk/handle/2438/31255?mode=full)). Fewer alloys means larger scrap pools with the same chemistry and less segregation cost.
- **Inverse design for scrap.** A Michigan Tech 2025 thesis used multi-objective Bayesian optimisation with Thermo-Calc on recycled Al-Mg-Fe-Si-Cu-Zn and achieved YS above 95 MPa as-cast ([MTU](https://digitalcommons.mtu.edu/etdr/2012)). The DFG also funds inverse design of wrought alloys with high cast-scrap content ([GEPRIS](https://gepris.dfg.de/gepris/projekt/562094916)).
- **Charge optimisation.** SMS Metallics Optimizer forecasts tramp elements before melting and picks the cheapest compliant mix ([SMS](https://www.sms-group.com/product/metallics-optimizer)). GHI ALEA claims up to **20% shorter cycle time and lower gas use** ([GHI](https://www.ghifurnaces.com/digitalize/)). Novamet SmartMelt (TMS 2025) claims up to 10% efficiency gain. **No vendor publishes a verified EUR/t saving.** The NSF SBIR project puts the US industry-wide value of better scrap-composition information at about US$1bn ([NSF](https://nsf.elsevierpure.com/en/projects/sbir-phase-ii-artificially-intelligent-solution-to-maximize-value-2/)).

### 5. Valuing impurity tolerance

Planning basis: LME is EUR 2,155/t, so **1 point of LME is EUR 21.55/t**. Public benchmarks are thin. The Fastmarkets Taint/Tabor assessment (MB-AL-0426, priced as % of LME, launched November 2025) is paywalled ([Fastmarkets](https://www.fastmarkets.com/insights/fastmarkets-launches-european-aluminium-scrap-shredded-old-sheet-taint-tabor-price-assessment/)). One vendor guide puts Rotterdam Taint/Tabor at 55–65% of LME, which is low reliability ([Nautica](https://nauticametalscrap.com/resources/aluminum-scrap-price-guide)). Recycling Europe disputes the Commission's "93% of LME" figure. The US UBC benchmark implies roughly 84% of primary in October 2026 (my calculation from search data). I use the board's 8–20 point grade spreads.

| Scenario (300 kt/yr casthouse output) | Mechanism | EUR/t of scrap affected | EUR m/yr |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Down-grade 100 kt of scrap intake by one grade (e.g. clean process scrap to sorted post-consumer old sheet) via wider Fe/Cu/Zn windows | 8–12 pts | 172–259 | **17–26** [est.] |
| B. Replace 30 kt of primary (10 pp more recycled content) with post-consumer scrap at about 80% LME | 20 pts, before freed primary sells at a premium (P1020 Rotterdam about US$460–510/t, [Fastmarkets](https://www.fastmarkets.com/insights/european-aluminium-billet-ingot-premium-spread-slowly-narrows/)) | ~431 | **~13** (up to ~25 if freed primary earns the premium) [est.] |
| C. AI charge optimisation: tighter aim-to-limit chemistry, 2–5 pp less sweetening with primary or alloying additions, 0.2–0.5 pp better melt yield | 3–5 pts on blended input | 5–15 per t output | **1.5–4.5** [est.] |
| D. Green premium on certified ≥75% post-consumer product, on 50 kt | Not public. CIRCAL pricing undisclosed | 50–200 [est.] | **2.5–10** [est.] |

The combined realistic range is **EUR 20–45m/yr [est.]**, with Scenario A the largest single item. CO2 effect of Scenario B: 30 kt × (16 − 0.5) t ≈ **465 kt CO2/yr avoided**.

### So what for our company

1. **Pair the 100 kt AI sorting hub with a "house" family of scrap-tolerant alloys.** Sorting without tolerant alloys still forces dilution with primary metal. Target 6xxx extrusion windows (Fe ≤0.5–0.8, Mn and Cr for α-phase) and an HRC 5xxx/6xxx sheet. This captures Scenario A, worth EUR 17–26m/yr.
2. **Deploy AI charge optimisation within 12 months.** Connect sorting-hub sensor data (LIBS/XRF) to the casthouse charge model. Payback should come in under 1 year [est.].
3. **Take a seat on the EN 573/EN 1706 revision committees.** Bring qualified HRC chemistries so that the new impurity windows match our scrap mix.
4. **Treat the crossover alloy as a partnership or licensing play, not a race.** AMAG has a lead of about 7 years. A 5/7 crossover fits our rolling mill, and Austrian proximity makes cooperation plausible.
5. **Launch a branded, DNV-verified ≥75% post-consumer product line** to meet automotive minimum recycled-content rules under the ELV Regulation and to compete with CIRCAL.

Qualification lead times are about **2–4 years for automotive sheet and extrusions** and about 1 year for building extrusions [est.]. Development should therefore start in 2027 to be ready for ELV recycled-content mandates.
