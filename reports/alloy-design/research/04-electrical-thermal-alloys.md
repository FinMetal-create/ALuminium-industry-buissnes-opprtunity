## Electrical Conductor, Battery & Thermal Management Alloys

*Research note for the CEO, 25 Oct 2026. Planning basis: LME normalised US$2,500/t (EUR 2,155/t); Cu/Al ratio ~4.9x. Source access: WebFetch failed with a DNS error on crugroup.com, fastmarkets.com and hellenicshippingnews.com, so figures from those sites come from search-engine extracts and the publishers' own press releases. Figures marked [est.] are my own reasoned estimates, each given as a range.*

### 1. Why this segment matters now

Electrification and AI data centres are now the fastest-growing outlet for conductor-grade and thermal-grade aluminium. Three forces are lining up:

- **Copper is very expensive relative to aluminium.** LME copper closed around US$14,540/t in mid-October 2026 ([Kedia daily report](https://investmentguruindia.com/editorial/uploads/news-pdf/52d0bcfd_Kedia%20Advisory%20Daily%20Basemetals%20Report%20As%20On%20(2).pdf); [S&P Global corrections 15 Oct 2026](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/pricing-benchmarks/our-methodology/corrections/20261015)). Aluminium was around US$3,000–3,135/t ([AlCircle](https://www.alcircle.com/news/lme-aluminium-offer-price-rises-to-3-134-5-t-as-stocks-remain-unchanged-at-240-375t-121456)), which puts the spot Cu/Al ratio at about 4.6–4.8x. The ratio hit a record 4.3 in January 2026 ([AlCircle](https://www.alcircle.com/news/ferrari-bmw-tesla-and-chinese-ev-makers-increase-aluminium-wiring-adoption-amid-rising-copper-costs-120146)), and our board basis assumes 4.9x. Industry puts the switching threshold at 3.5–4.0x, and Nexans quotes about 3.5x ([AlCircle](https://www.alcircle.com/news/rising-copper-prices-accelerate-shift-towards-aluminium-wiring-in-automotive-industry-120125)). The ratio is now well above that threshold.
- **Grids.** The EU Grid Action Plan estimates EUR 584bn of grid investment is needed this decade ([European Commission](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/publications/eu-action-plan-grids_en)). Cross-border capacity is set to grow by 23 GW by 2025 and a further 64 GW by 2030 ([Enerdata](https://www.enerdata.net/publications/daily-energy-news/eu-commission-unveils-its-action-plan-upgrade-power-grids-europe.html)). Overhead lines are almost entirely aluminium.
- **AI data centres.**
  - Wood Mackenzie puts peak aluminium demand *inside* data centres at 0.6–0.9 Mt/yr. About 55% of that is cooling and about 25% is racking and enclosures. Across the whole system, including grid connection and on-site generation, metal demand is 3–4x the in-asset volume ([Wood Mackenzie](https://www.woodmac.com/press-releases/wood-mackenzie-data-centre-metals-demand-isnt-an-asset-story.-its-a-grid-story); [Northern Miner](https://www.northernminer.com/news/the-metals-ai-data-centres-need-most-arent-inside-them/1003892172/)).
  - CRU expects copper plus aluminium data-centre demand of about 850–900 kt in 2026, up roughly 25% year on year ([CRU, via search extract](https://www.crugroup.com/en/communities/thought-leadership/2026/cru-expands-long-term-data-centre-metallics-forecasting/)).
  - Data-centre-driven power additions are expected to rise from 15–20 GW/yr today to 30–33 GW/yr in the early 2030s.

Global aluminium wire-rod use reached about **8.32 Mt in 2025**, up from 7.6 Mt in 2020. Bare conductors account for 70–75% of it, and China and India together account for about 68% ([AlCircle](https://www.aluminiumchina.com/en-gb/news-center/industry-news/2026/3/3.html)).

### 2. Cu→Al substitution economics at ~4.9x

Aluminium has about 61% of copper's conductivity, so it needs about 1.6x the cross-section to carry the same current ([Skillings](https://skillings.net/copper-deficit-impact-2026-why-substitution-is-not-a-short-term-fix/)). Because aluminium is much lighter (2.70 vs 8.96 g/cm³), the aluminium conductor still weighs only about **0.48–0.50x** the copper one. In practice, **1 t of aluminium replaces about 2.0 t of copper** in current-carrying duty.

On the board basis (Al EUR 2,155/t, Cu at 4.9x ≈ EUR 10,560/t):

- The copper metal replaced by 1 t of aluminium costs about EUR 21,000. The aluminium costs EUR 2,155.
- That is a **metal saving of about EUR 19,000 per tonne of aluminium deployed** [est.].
- On metal alone, the two would cost the same at a Cu/Al ratio of only about 2.0x.

The real-world threshold is 3.5–4.0x because aluminium cables cost more in other ways: more insulation and sheath, bigger cable trays, special connectors, and a terminations and creep risk. Even after those costs, cable-level savings at 4.9x are probably **25–45%** for low- and medium-voltage power cable [est.].

Where adoption stands:

- **Cable makers.** Prysmian says aluminium is now about 40% of its conductor material by weight, up 3 percentage points over five years. Nexans expects aluminium to take a larger share of grid projects ([AlCircle](https://www.alcircle.com/news/rising-copper-prices-accelerate-shift-towards-aluminium-wiring-in-automotive-industry-120125)).
- **Cars.** BMW (since 2011), Tesla (Model Y, Cybertruck), Ferrari (up to 20% lighter wiring), Stellantis, XPeng, Xiaomi and AVATR are all expanding aluminium wiring ([TimesLive/Reuters](https://www.timeslive.co.za/motoring/2026-06-30-ferrari-and-bmw-follow-tesla-and-chinese-brands-in-shift-to-cheaper-aluminium-wiring/)).
- **Transformers.** ABB reports more than 1,500 power transformers above 10 MVA built with aluminium windings ([IEEMA paper](https://cablewire2026.ieema.org/)). Copper-clad and hybrid windings are gaining attention ([Drives&Controls, Aug 2026](https://drivesncontrols.com/aluminium-an-attractive-alternative-to-copper-windings/)).
- **Limits.** Aluminium remains constrained where space is tight (GPU racks, dense switchgear) and in building wire under European codes. A 2017 copper-industry study expected EU standards to "severely restrict" AA-8000 building wire ([ICA](https://internationalcopper.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/2017.04-Aluminum-AA8000-Factsheet.pdf)). 8xxx alloys hold about 72% of aluminium building wire globally ([pmarketresearch, low reliability](https://pmarketresearch.com/worldwide-aluminum-building-wire-market-research)).

A recent patent (EP4124668) targets HPDC rotor alloys with conductivity of 23 MS/m or more (preferably over 30) and yield strength (Rp0.2) of 74 MPa or more (preferably over 95). It compares them with Castasil-21, which reaches up to 28 MS/m at 80–100 MPa ([Justia](https://patents.justia.com/patent/20230043878)).

**Overhead conductors.** Al-Zr conductors run at 150–200 °C, against 75 °C for ACSR ([Acta Physica Polonica](https://przyrbwn.icm.edu.pl/APP/ABSTR/127/a127-4-120.html)). That lets utilities raise the capacity of existing corridors without re-permitting, which matters when EU high-voltage permits take 8–10 years. Market participants:

- **Nexans** sells Lo-Sag and HVCRC carbon-core conductors, which carry up to 2x the current of ACSR ([Nexans](https://www.nexans.fr/en/products/Power-Infrastructure/Overhead-lines/Advanced-conductors/Lo-Sag/ACPR/ACPS-LoSag38429/product~ID540490761~.html)).
- **CTC Global** claims ACCC on more than 50,000 km in 65+ countries, with 2025 projects in Europe ([CTC Global](https://ctcglobal.com/?p=266860)).
- **Lamifil** (Belgium) holds a patented Al alloy with additions from groups 3, 4 or 5 or the lanthanides, heat-treated at 185–315 °C to reach 61% IACS or more ([Justia](https://patents.justia.com/assignee/lamifil-n-v)).
- **Midal** sells MiRecAL 6101/6201 rod with 15% and 30% pre-consumer recycled content ([EPD](https://www.environdec.com/library/epd28974)).
- **Hydro** Karmøy supplies about 70 kt/yr of EC wire rod to Europe ([Hydro](https://hydro.com/en/aluminium/products/casthouse-products/wire-rod-for-cable-and-wire-solutions/)).

**Battery foil.** Foil demand is about 250 t/GWh as a general assumption ([Hindalco/EVreporter](https://evreporter.com/hindalco-set-to-localise-battery-foils-for-lithium-ion-cells-this-year/)) and about 350 t/GWh for LFP ([Shyam Metalics](https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/2023/09/27/shyam-metalics-to-make-aluminum-foil-for-lithium-ion-cells)). Sodium-ion cells use aluminium on both electrodes, which roughly doubles demand. European cell capacity in 2030 has been cut back to 1.1–1.5 TWh theoretical ([Fraunhofer ISI](https://publica.fraunhofer.de/handle/publica/484103)), and realised output is more likely 400–900 GWh [est.]. That implies **100–300 kt/yr of EU battery foil demand by 2030** [est.]. Gränges spent SEK 600m to double cathode-foil capacity in Finspång, coming online in 2025 ([Gränges](https://mfn.se/a/granges/granges-invests-in-increased-battery-foil-production-in-finspang)). China added about 1.27 Mt of foil capacity across 17 projects ([AlCircle](https://www.alcircle.com/news/growing-demand-for-aluminium-battery-foil-prompts-china-to-commission-17-new-capacities-98896)), so conversion margins are under pressure.

**Thermal management.** Aluminium cold-plate market forecasts conflict:

- About US$0.86bn (2024) rising to US$3.6bn (2032), including EV battery plates ([report summary](https://siddhiblog.odoo.com/blog/news-2/aluminum-for-cold-plate-market-global-outlook-and-forecast-2025-2032-19778)).
- About US$2.18bn in Asia-Pacific alone in 2025 ([HTF](https://www.htfmarketintelligence.com/report/asia-pacific-aluminum-cold-plate-market)).
- Data-centre liquid cooling overall: US$3.5–11bn by 2030 depending on the source ([ABI](https://abiresearch.com/news-resources/chart-data/data-center-liquid-cooling-market-size); [Arizton](https://www.arizton.com/market-reports/data-center-liquid-cooling-market)).

GPU cold plates today are mostly copper. Aluminium wins in manifolds, CDUs, rack-level heat exchangers, immersion tanks and heat sinks, and the open technical question is coolant corrosion ([UT Arlington thesis](https://mavmatrix.uta.edu/mechaerospace_theses/1036)).

### 4. Where AI-optimised alloys create an edge

The central metallurgical problem in every family above is the **strength vs conductivity (electrical or thermal) trade-off**. Electrical and thermal conductivity are very sensitive to elements held in solid solution, especially Ti, V, Cr and Mn, and also Fe, Si and Cu. Those are exactly the elements that rise when more scrap goes into the melt.

- **ML-driven design is well suited to this trade-off.**
  - A Beijing Institute of Technology group combined ML with thermodynamic (CALPHAD) phase inputs to design high-strength conductive alloys ([BIT](https://pure.bit.edu.cn/en/publications/design-of-high-strength-and-electrically-conductive-aluminium-all/)).
  - ML-designed Al-2.64Si-0.43Mg alloys reached thermal conductivity above 190 W/mK with UTS above 220 MPa, close to the model predictions ([J. Mater. Informatics 2024](https://www.oaepublish.com/articles/jmi.2024.21)).
- **The best published conductor wires** reach about 195 MPa at 61.2% IACS (Al-0.2Zr-0.06Sc, [Mater. Res. Lett. 2024](https://doaj.org/article/a7149dddea5e48b9b7cdddad5d18a941); [HIT](https://scholar.hit.edu.cn/en/publications/enhancement-of-strength-and-electrical-conductivity-for-a-dilute-/)). Severe-deformation Al-Fe reaches 268 MPa but only about 45% IACS ([KIT](https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000181180/159360113)). Getting to 250 MPa or more at 60% IACS or more, with Sc kept low, is the "holy grail" for HTLS and busbars.
- **Industrial AI alloy design is still early in Europe.**
  - Alloyed and BCAST (Brunel) have a £525k EPSRC programme on scrap-based alloys, including a heat-conducting alloy ([The Engineer](https://www.theengineer.co.uk/content/news/525k-to-accelerate-ai-alloy-design)).
  - TECNALIA runs the MetalIA project (Apr 2026) ([TECNALIA](https://www.tecnalia.com/en/news/transforming-traditional-metallurgy-by-integrating-artificial-intelligence)).
  - Phaseshift Technologies licenses AI-designed alloys ([Draper](https://www.draper.vc/portfolio/phaseshift)).
  - I found no public AI conductor-alloy programme at Hydro, Novelis or Constellium, which leaves room for a first mover.
- **Development economics.** Classical development of a conductor alloy takes 3–5 years. ML plus high-throughput casting can shorten screening to 6–18 months, at about EUR 1–3m per alloy family [est.]. After that comes qualification:
  - utility type tests (IEC 62641/EN 50183/IEC 62004) and pilot lines: 1.5–3 years [est.];
  - battery-foil customer qualification: 12–24 months [est.];
  - data-centre thermal OEM approval: 6–12 months [est.].

### 6. So what for our company

1. **We have the hard-to-copy assets.** Our smelter supplies hot metal directly to the casthouse, the same set-up Midal and Hydro Karmøy use, which avoids remelt cost and dross. Hot metal is also the cheapest route to EC-grade rod with low Ti and V after boron treatment. Few EU producers can do this, and EU conductor makers (Nexans, Prysmian, NKT, Lamifil, Tratos, De Angeli) import a large share of their rod from Midal (Gulf), India and Norway.
2. **The sorting hub and 8xxx/6xxx conductors fit together.** 8030/8176 deliberately contain 0.3–1.0% Fe, and 6101/6201 tolerate controlled Si and Mg. AI-sorted wire-and-cable scrap (EN 13920-3) and clean 6xxx extrusion scrap can make up 30–50% of conductor melts. That would undercut Midal's 15–30% MiRecAL benchmark and bring conductor CO₂ toward 4–8 t/t [est.]. The sorting hub's scrap-grade spread of 8–20 points of LME (EUR 170–430/t) then becomes conductor margin instead of exported scrap.
3. **Use AI for impurity-tolerant conductor chemistry**, not for exotic alloys. The aim is to model conductivity as a function of the scrap mix, boron treatment and ageing, then guarantee 61% IACS with a high recycled share. That turns the EU's record 1.27 Mt of scrap exports into a domestic premium product.
4. **Play to strengths downstream.** Busbars, busway and thermal extrusions for data centres are a near-term fit for our extrusion business. Battery foil is capital-intensive and heavily contested by Chinese capacity, so we should supply foil stock or partner rather than build a full foil line. Rotor alloys are a small niche.
5. **Qualify early.** Grid TSOs, data-centre OEMs and cable makers each have 1–3 year qualification cycles. Starting type tests in 2027 positions us for the 2028–2032 peak in EU reconductoring and data-centre builds.
