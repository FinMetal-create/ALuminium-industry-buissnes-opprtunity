## Advanced Alloys: AM, Scandium, Al-Li, Al-Ce & Nano-Modified

*Research note, 25 Oct 2026. FX follows the board planning basis: US$2,500 = EUR 2,155, so 1 EUR = 1.16 USD. "[est.]" marks an analyst estimate. Method note: direct fetches of pubs.usgs.gov, news.mit.edu and cruxinvestor.com failed on DNS errors in this environment. The figures from those sites below come from search-engine extracts of the same documents. The shared web-search budget ran out before I could check Er/Yb substitution studies or the EU Critical Raw Materials Act (CRMA) strategic-project list, so the gaps are flagged.*

### 1. Why this niche matters: small tonnage, high value per tonne
Every alloy family in this note is tiny next to the company's ~300 kt/yr. Their value comes from price per kg, not volume. For reference, LME metal is about EUR 2.2/kg. AM powder sells for EUR 70-125/kg, Al-Sc master alloy for about EUR 85/kg, and scandium oxide for about EUR 510-690/kg. Our advantage would be owning the melt chemistry, the casthouse and soon a sorted-scrap stream, then selling into these premium niches. We should not try to beat incumbents at their own processes.

### 2. Additive manufacturing (AM) alloys, powder and WAAM wire
**Market.** AM Research puts the 2025 3D-printing market at US$16 bn, of which US$6.27 bn is metals ([Digital Engineering / AM Research](https://www.digitalengineering247.com/article/3d-printing-markets-reach-16-billion-in-2025/materials)). SmarTech, AM Research's former name, put aluminium at only ~3% of metal AM in 2018 and ~4% in 2023 ([AM&D](https://aerospacemanufacturinganddesign.com/article/additive-manufacturing-metal-powder)). Vendor numbers disagree badly. HTF puts North American AM aluminium powder at US$420 m in 2025, growing 16.7% a year ([HTF](https://www.htfmarketintelligence.com/report/north-america-aluminum-powder-for-additive-manufacturing-market)). The same vendor puts North American aluminium WAAM materials at US$680 m ([HTF](https://www.htfmarketintelligence.com/report/north-america-aluminum-wire-arc-additive-manufacturing-materials-market)). Both look inflated. My own triangulation: global aluminium AM powder of **~1-3 kt/yr worth ~EUR 100-250 m, growing 12-18% a year [est.]**.

**Price.** Standard AlSi10Mg powder (30-60 µm) from North American suppliers cost US$135-145/kg in 2024. A lower-cost alternative, NExP-1, cost ~US$85/kg ([UT Austin 2024](https://utw10945.utweb.utexas.edu/sites/default/files/2024/58%20Towards%20Affordable%20Aluminum%20Additive%20Manufactur.pdf)). The same alloy as ingot sold for ~US$3.1/kg ex-works China in January 2026 ([SMM](https://www.metal.com/en/prices/202308180002)). Atomising and classifying powder therefore multiplies value **25-45x**. Powder is usually <15% of the cost of a finished AM part ([Metal AM](https://www.metal-am.com/articles/scalmalloy-is-too-expensive-and-design-optimisation-only-makes-sense-in-aerospace/)), so buyers accept premium alloys.

**Named alloys and players**
- **Constellium Aheadd CP1 (Al-Fe-Zr).** Contains no rare earths, no Si and no volatile elements. Industrialised in 2020, approved for Formula 1 from the 2024 season, and qualified by Burloak after a year-long programme. Qualloy distributes it in Europe from stock in Germany ([Constellium](https://www.constellium.com/news/aheadd-r-cp1-constelliums-high-performance-aluminium-additive-manufacturing-powder-approved-for-use-on-formula1-racing-cars), [Metal AM](https://www.metal-am.com/?p=117389)). This is the closest template for an EU primary producer.
- **Scalmalloy (Al-Mg-Sc, Airbus APWORKS).** Sold only through licensed producers: Toyal Europe and LPW, plus a 2025 MoU with Equispheres for North America ([APWORKS](https://www.apworks.de/scalmalloy-powder), [3DPI](https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/north-american-scalmalloy-production-begins-with-apworks-and-equispheres-238868/)).
- **HRL 7A77.** Nano-functionalised 7075. The Citrine platform screened ~11.5 m powder and nanoparticle combinations to find it ([Citrine](https://citrine.io/first-to-market-high-strength-3d-printable-aluminum-alloy/)). It was the first AM alloy registered with the Aluminum Association ([HRL](https://www.hrl.com/tag/aluminum-association)).
- **Elementum 3D A6061-RAM2 / A7050-RAM2.** Reactive in-situ inoculants. Deposition rate is ~50% higher than AlSi10Mg ([datasheet](https://mma.prnewswire.com/media/2339000/A6061_RAM2_Data_Sheet.pdf?p=pdf), [3printr](https://www.3printr.com/elementum-3d-a7050-ram2-receives-approval-from-the-aluminum-association-0571794/)).
- **A20X.** Registered as 2A05.50 ([AL Circle](https://www.alcircle.com/news/aluminum-association-accepts-the-registration-of-a20x-with-new-metal-powder-designations-60747)).
- **NanoAL Addalloy.** Zr-based and Sc-free. NanoAL belongs to Unity Aluminum, and Mitsubishi RtM licenses the alloy for Asia ([steel-technology](https://www.steel-technology.com/index.php/pressreleases/nanoal-a-wholly-owned-subsidiary-of-unity-aluminum-signs-a-long-term-commercial-license-agreement-with-mitsubishi-corporation-rtm-japan-for-advanced-aluminum-alloy-products)).
- **6K Additive.** Makes plasma powders from certified machining scrap. It says ~70% of material is lost in conventional powder making ([Metal AM](https://www.metal-am.com/?p=73653)).

**AI angle.** MIT and CMU (Taheri-Mousavi) used ML with Bayesian optimisation to design a printable Al-Er-Zr-Y-Yb-Ni alloy. They evaluated **40 compositions instead of >1 m simulations**. The printed alloy is ~5x stronger than cast aluminium and ~50% stronger than the best printable benchmark after ageing at 400 °C ([arXiv 2406.17457](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.17457v1), [EurekAlert / MIT, Oct 2025](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1101044)). The design loop has dropped from years to months. Qualification has not: aerospace AM qualification still takes **3-7 years [est.]**.

### 3. Scandium and Sc-lean substitutes
**Supply and price.** USGS estimates 2025 world consumption of Sc2O3 at ~60 t. China added scandium metal, alloys, oxides and compounds to its export controls in April 2025. They remained in force in December 2025, with general licences issued only to selected exporters ([USGS MCS 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-scandium.pdf), via search extract). One estimate puts ~85% of refined supply under Chinese control ([Crux](https://www.cruxinvestor.com/posts/filling-the-scandium-vacuum-left-by-china-export-restrictions)). Prices:
- 99.99% oxide averaged ~US$640/kg in 2025 (USGS), about **EUR 550/kg**.
- The SMM benchmark was US$591/kg on 3 Sep 2026, with spot quotes up to US$801/kg ([tracker](https://rare-earth-mining.com/scandium-price/)).
- The US Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) plans to buy 6.4 t from Rio Tinto for up to US$40 m over five years. That implies ~US$6,250/kg, a strategic premium ([Kitco/Reuters](https://www.kitco.com/news/off-the-wire/2025-09-22/us-agency-wants-buy-scandium-oxide-rio-tinto-defence-stockpile), [MWI](https://mwi.westpoint.edu/the-sixty-ton-problem-scandium-supply-chain-risk-the-us-defense-industrial-base/)).

**Western projects**
- **Rio Tinto, Sorel-Tracy.** 3 t/yr from TiO2 waste streams. Cost US$6 m and was built in under six months ([Rio Tinto](https://www.riotinto.com/en/can/news/releases/2021/rio-tinto-opens-new-scandium-plant-in-canada)). Output has stayed below nameplate because demand is thin.
- **Sunrise Syerston.** 60 t/yr, commissioning targeted for 1H 2028, with expansion potential to 180 t/yr. It has a US$400 m conditional loan from the US Office of Strategic Capital. Lockheed Martin has a non-binding option on 15 t/yr ([ASX](https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20260810/pdf/072l1673ycxhhl.pdf), [Kalkine](https://kalkine.com.au/news/stocks/lockheed-martin-secures-option-for-25-of-sunrise-scandium-output-in-australia)).
- **Rusal, Bogoslovsky.** 1.55 t/yr from red mud, scalable to 19 t/yr ([Interfax](https://www.interfax.com/newsroom/top-stories/110059/)). It is sanctions-exposed and not an option for us.

**EU red-mud scandium.** Under the Horizon 2020 SCALE project, Metlen (Aluminium of Greece) leached >10 t of bauxite residue at pilot scale and reached concentrates of up to 25 wt% Sc. The project produced Sc2O3, ScF3 and Al-Sc master alloy. Metlen's 850 kt/yr of residue carries **70-80 t/yr of Sc**, more than today's global supply ([ICSOBA 2021](https://icsoba.org/assets/files/publications/2021/Shorts/BR11S%20-%20Developing%20a%20Parallel-to-Aluminium%20Value%20Chain%20for%20Scandium%20and%20Al-Sc%20Alloy%20Production.%20Pilot%20Scale%20Results%20under%20the%20SCALE%20Project.pdf), [CORDIS](https://cordis.europa.eu/article/id/436238-elusive-scandium-awaits-in-industrial-by-products)). A life-cycle assessment found the acid leach accounts for ≥80% of the route's environmental impact ([FHNW LCA](https://irf.fhnw.ch/entities/publication/9f3b855d-ab84-4f49-93b5-a401d60e074c)). I found no 2025-26 announcement of a commercial plant. I could not verify the "ScaVanger" project.

**Demand split.** Solid oxide fuel cells, mainly Bloom Energy, take ~40-67% of scandium. Al-Sc alloys take ~20-30% ([MMTA](https://mmta.co.uk/scandium-emerges-from-the-shadows/), [Asian Metal](https://www.asianmetal.com/Executive-Interviews/Scandium-oxide-production-capacity-to-further-grow-Interview-with-Donghong-Nie-General-Manager-Hunan-Oriental-Scandium-Co-Ltd.shtml)).

**Master alloy economics.** Al-2%Sc sells for ~US$98/kg. Global production is ~855 t/yr ([Valuates](https://reports.valuates.com/market-reports/QYRE-Auto-34R6611/global-aluminium-scandium)). The oxide contained in 1 t of Al-2Sc is ~30.7 kg Sc2O3, worth ~US$20/kg of master alloy at US$640/kg oxide. Conversion therefore captures **~US$60-75/kg**. FEA Materials claims it can make Al-2Sc for <US$33/kg if oxide costs US$750/kg ([TMS 2025 abstract](https://www.programmaster.org/PM/PM.nsf/ApprovedAbstracts/A2B7CF0951FD3EBB85258B4A006127E9)). At 0.2% Sc, the scandium alone adds **~EUR 8,000-8,500/t** to the alloy, about four times the metal price. This is why Sc-lean design matters.

**Sc-lean substitutes.** Zr stabilises and partly replaces Sc in the L12 Al3(Sc,Zr) precipitates. Er and Yb have been shown to sit in the same phase, as in the MIT/CMU alloy above. ML is well suited to mapping these multi-element trade-offs. The search budget ran out before I could retrieve recent Er/Yb cost studies, so this point rests on the MIT/CMU and NanoAL evidence.

### 4. Aluminium-lithium
The Al-Li market is ~US$2.3-2.8 bn ([DataM](https://www.datamintelligence.com/research-report/aluminum-lithium-alloys-market), [Valuates](https://reports.valuates.com/market-reports/QYRE-Auto-28I11340/global-aluminium-lithium-alloy)). Constellium's Airware is used on the A350, A220 and Falcon 9. Its Issoire casthouse started with 14 kt/yr in 2013. Two more casthouses and Airware-specific recycling were planned to nearly triple output, for €52 m in total ([AIN](https://oldain.ainonline.com/aviation-news/aerospace/2013-04-08/constellium-set-meet-aluminum-lithium-demand), [PRN](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/constellium-to-build-two-new-casthouses-in-its-issoire-france-plant-to-accelerate-production-and-recycling-of-its-airware-aerospace-technology-254303581.html)). Alloy 2050 is ~5% less dense than 7050-T7451 at an "acceptable extra cost per kilo saved" ([J. Mater. Eng. Perf.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11665-009-9554-Z)). I found no public per-tonne premium. My estimate is that Al-Li plate sells at **~1.5-2.5x the price of 7xxx plate [est.]**.

Demand is tied to aircraft build rates. Airbus now expects A320 output of 70-75 per month by end-2027 ([GlobalAir](https://www.globalair.com/articles/airbus-reports-2025-financial-results-a320-production-target-revised/11971)). Producers need Li-safe casting, airframer qualification of 5-10 years [est.], and supply contracts already held by Constellium, Arconic/Howmet and Novelis. **Making Al-Li ourselves is not investable.** Sorting Al-Li scrap is: lithium-bearing scrap contaminates ordinary scrap streams, and spectroscopic (LIBS) sorting can separate it.

### 5. Al-Ce and high-temperature alloys
ORNL and Eck Industries developed Al-Ce alloys with 400 MPa UTS and 80% retention at 240 °C, with stability up to ~300-500 °C. Many applications need no heat treatment, a step the developers say can cost 50-60% as much as the casting itself ([ASM](https://www.asminternational.org/hts/?p=8577), [DOE EERE](https://www.energy.gov/eere/success-stories/articles/eere-success-story-taking-aluminum-alloys-new-heights)). Eck sells a printable grade, DuAlumin-3D ([3DPrint](https://3dprint.com/320336/ornl-continues-with-dualumin-3d/)). Cerium is a by-product in surplus from NdPr magnet mining and costs only ~US$1.2-3.9/kg ([IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/cerium-oxide-price-trend), [EMR](https://www.expertmarketresearch.com/price-forecast/cerium-price-trends)). ORNL's ACMZ alloy (Al-Cu-Mn-Zr) runs ~100 °C hotter than A356/319. It was developed in under four years, and Nemak cast more than 120 variants into production-design cylinder heads ([ORNL](https://www.ornl.gov/blog/ornl-developed-alloy-promises-better-fuel-economy)). I found no public 2024-26 production news for either alloy.

### 6. Nano-modified alloys, MMCs and high-entropy alloys
The global metal matrix composite (MMC) market is ~US$490-920 m, about 46% of it aluminium-matrix. AlSiC alone is ~US$54 m ([Mordor](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/metal-matrix-composites-market), [Reportprime](https://www.reportprime.com/aluminum-silicon-carbide-alsic-r4965.md)). Nano-inoculation (HRL 7A77, Elementum 3D's RAM process) matters most as a crack-suppression tool for AM and welding, not as a tonnage business. High-entropy alloys have **low relevance** for an aluminium producer before 2035. Lightweight HEAs are still at the academic stage.

### So what for our company
1. **Sell elements and chemistry, not parts.** The defensible plays are Sc-lean, Zr/Er-modified extrusion and conductor alloys, and an EU Al-Sc/Zr master alloy made from non-Chinese oxide. Both use the existing casthouse and need low capex.
2. **Use AI on alloys that tolerate scrap.** ML-designed alloys with Fe and Zr, like the Aheadd CP1 template, can absorb iron from the 100 kt AI-sorted scrap stream. That turns a grade spread of 8-20 LME points into a margin source.
3. **Enter AM through licensing or tolling, not a large atomiser.** The market is ~1-3 kt globally. A 300-500 t/yr position at EUR 70-100/kg would earn about **EUR 25-50 m in revenue [est.]**.
4. **Use the sorting hub to segregate Al-Li and Sc-bearing aerospace scrap** and sell it in closed loops at a premium.
5. **Do not enter Al-Li or HEAs.** Keep Al-Ce and ACMZ-type high-temperature foundry alloys as low-cost options to offer foundry customers.
