## 2. One more sized instrument: the Innovation Fund small-scale category

- **Rules:** the small-scale category covers projects with capex of EUR 2.5-20 m. Relevant costs are the total capex, and the grant can cover up to 60% of them (Innovation Fund general-call rules).
- **Fit:** Case B, the NHT HPDC casthouse, has EUR 18 m capex. The maximum grant is 0.6 × 18 = **EUR 10.8 m**.
- **Competitive request:** a bid of 30-40% scores better on cost-efficiency, which means **EUR 5.4-7.2 m**.
- **Expected value:** at a 15-25% success rate [est.], that is about **EUR 0.8-1.8 m**. The memo assumes EUR 3.6 m, which is defensible only if we bid.
- **Threshold trap:** the memo's 10% contingency takes B to EUR 19.8 m. Any overrun above EUR 20 m moves the project into the medium-scale category. There, relevant costs become the extra cost against a reference plant, which would cut the eligible base sharply.
  - **Action:** cap B's filed capex below EUR 20 m.
- **GHG case (needs the alloy to tolerate Fe so it can take scrap):** 30 kt/yr × 50% post-consumer scrap × (16 − 0.5) t CO2/t = **232 kt CO2/yr**, or 2.3 Mt over 10 years. EUR 7.2 m / 2.3 Mt ≈ EUR 3/t CO2, which is very competitive [est.]. The Innovation Fund's methodology will probably use a narrower reference than 16 t. Even at 8 t the figure is about EUR 6/t.
- **Next call:** IF26, opening around Dec 2026 with a deadline around Apr 2027 [est.]. **Owner: Grants Office and the Recycling Hub lead.**
- **RFCS: not eligible.** The Research Fund for Coal and Steel is limited to the coal and steel sectors (Council Decision 2008/376/EC). Only an aluminium-steel joining work package run with a steel partner could qualify. Book EUR 0.
- **IPCEI-CAM: unverified.** I cannot confirm that a notified IPCEI covers aluminium materials. Book EUR 0 in the base case. If a national pre-notification exists, past per-company awards have been EUR 10-100 m [est.]. **Owner: Government Affairs, ask the ministry.**

## 4. Section 3 decisions, one each

| Family | Decision | Reasoning and calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Al-Ce | **WATCH** | Niche high-temperature castings, and Ce supply depends on China. Spend no more than EUR 0.3 m, and only as a casting partner in a Horizon consortium. **Upgrade trigger:** an OEM asks for a specification and Ce comes from a non-Chinese source |
| Certified low-carbon / recycled-content brand | **GO** | About EUR 1-2 m for ASI chain-of-custody, EPDs and the brand. Product premium is EUR 50-150/t [est.], while the premium on ingot is close to zero. 50 kt × EUR 75/t = EUR 3.75 m/yr, so payback is under 1 year. It is also needed to win OEM Scope 3 tenders |
| Al-Sc | **NO-GO** | 0.3% Sc = 3 kg Sc/t = 4.6 kg Sc2O3. At US$1,000/kg that adds about **EUR 4,000/t** in material alone, and China export-controlled Sc from 4 Apr 2025 ([USGS MCS 2025](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-scandium.pdf)). Revisit only if supply from bauxite residue is secured |
| AM tolling | **NO-GO** | The global market for aluminium AM powder is a few kt [est.], and established players already cover it (Equispheres, Elementum 3D, ECKART, Colibrium). It does not fit a 300 kt producer |
| Twin-roll casting | **WATCH** | Capex is about EUR 30-60 m against more than EUR 300 m for a DC-cast plus hot-mill route [est.]. The product mix is limited (foil stock and 3xxx/8xxx). **GO trigger:** toll trials (EUR 0.2-0.4 m, using the MAT-PROD-22 pilot line) show 5xxx/6xxx meeting specification with 60% or more hub scrap |
