## 3. Evidence for the A4 premium (€/t)

| Basis | Calculation | €/t |
|---|---|---|
| Exchange low-carbon differential | US$10–40/t ÷ 1.16 (repo 08) | 9–34 |
| Branded bilateral premium (CIRCAL- or REDUXA-type) | repo 07 [est.]; repo r2-07 high case €100/t | 50–300 |
| Customer CO2 value ceiling, building | (6.7 − 2.0) kg/kg = 4.7 t CO2/t Al × €50–100/t [est.] | 235–470 |
| Customer CO2 value ceiling, Volvo | 4.7 × €90 | ~423 |
| Board basis, 75% recycled-content alloy versus primary | (16 − (0.75×0.5 + 0.25×16)) = 11.6 t CO2/t × €90 | ~1,050 (theoretical) |

The 6.7 kg CO2e/kg figure for EU average purchased metal is an assumption [est.]. The Board-basis row is theoretical only; it shows that customers are not paying their full CO2 value.

**Recommended A4 premium:**
- Building systems: **base €100/t (range €50–200)**.
- Crash management: **base €175/t (range €100–300)**. This is justified by a tighter CO2 and recycled-content specification and by Volvo's internal carbon price.

**Sizing (assumptions):**
- **Named pool:** 200–310 kt (B1–B5) plus about 60–100 kt (C1–C3 CMS) [est.].
- **Capture:** 8% of building and 10% of automotive by year 3, which is about 20 kt plus about 8 kt.
- **Premium pool:** 20 kt × €100 + 8 kt × €175 = **€3.4m/yr** (range 1.4–6.4).
- **Main risk:** the incumbent (Hydro CIRCAL at Schüco). Do not base the case on B1.
