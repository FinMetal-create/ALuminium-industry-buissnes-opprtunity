**Slovalco 2023 tonnage [est.]:** €99.5M of sales ÷ about €2,400-2,700/t for billet or foundry product gives **37-41 kt**. If part of that was toll remelting, physical tonnage could be up to ~55 kt. That is about 20-28% of the casthouse and 21-31% of former primary.

## 2. Ratios for the board

Two ratios matter: solid-melt capacity divided by former primary liquid, and solid-melt capacity divided by casthouse.

- **Rheinwerk (best case):** 166 ÷ 160 = **~104%** of former primary, and 38% of the ingot permit. The permitted solid melt of 370.5 kt is 85% of the casthouse. It took two furnace projects (2016 and 2026) and €85M. Rheinwerk also has an unusual advantage: a scrap loop from the neighbouring Alunorf mill (liquid metal goes there by crucible).
- **Trimet Essen (integrated, full potlines):** 100 ÷ 165 = **61%**.
- **Slovalco and Aldel (stranded):** **21-31%** of former primary. Aldel ended in bankruptcy and Slovalco lost money. Scrap melting alone did not carry the fixed costs.
- **Our company:** 190 ÷ 300 = **63%** of primary liquid. That is at the level Trimet Essen runs every day, but well above what the stranded sites managed. It is reachable only with a configuration like Rheinwerk's: dedicated multi-chamber or side-well furnaces, scrap storage of 6.5-16 kt, and secured scrap supply.

**Yield check:** the S4 permit gives 66 ÷ 83 = **79.5%** metal yield on coated UBC. For 190 kt of liquid C we therefore need about **200 kt of scrap at 95% yield (clean process scrap)** up to **240 kt at 80% (post-consumer)** [est.]. That is 16-19% of the 1.27 Mt the EU exported in 2025. Press coverage of Speira's new furnace says the next bottleneck there is scrap supply, not furnace capacity.

## 4. What C ≥ 190 kt implies [est.]

We have no public figure for our current solid-melt capacity. Integrated casthouses run 25-40% solid charge (Essen ~38%, Aldel 28%). On an assumed ~330 kt casthouse, that suggests **existing solid melt of 80-130 kt**.

- **Gap to 190 kt:** 60-110 kt.
- **Capex:** 60 × €600 = **€36M** (low); 85 × €800 = **€68M** (central); 110 × €1,000 = **€110M** (high).
- **Downside case:** if C stays at Slovalco-like levels (~30% of primary, about 90-130 kt), the "robust under curtailment" figures (Case A €78.7M; portfolio €67.2M; complements €70.8-89.9M) need re-running at C = 100-130 kt.

**Board message:** the claim is credible only as a funded configuration (about €68M central capex, 200-240 kt of scrap secured, FID 2027). It is not a passive outcome. One analogue reached it (Rheinwerk), one integrated site runs at that level (Trimet Essen), and two stranded sites fell far short and failed (Slovalco, Aldel).
