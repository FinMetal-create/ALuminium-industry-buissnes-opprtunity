## 2. Verified ladder for grades that can feed billet and sheet (A3)

| Grade | Range | Central |
|---|---|---|
| Post-consumer 95/5 6063 / LIBS-sorted 6xxx | 87-90 | **89** (anchored on the Fastmarkets print) |
| Taint/tabor | 78-85 | **82** (anchored on Recycling Europe and Argus) |
| Old rolled / mixed wrought | 80-86 | **83** [est.] |

**Blend.** I assume A3's 15 kt splits 50/50 between billet and sheet [est.]:
- Billet uses sorted 6063 at 89.
- Sheet uses taint/tabor and old rolled at an average of 82.5.
- The blend is about **85% of LME**, with a range of 82.5-87.5.

**Gross spread = 100 − 85 = 15 points (12.5-17.5).** The memo's 80% (20 points) holds only for twitch, Zorba and shred. The memo itself says those grades need the hub.

## 3. Net substitution spread, on the memo's basis (primary valued at LME)

The scrap replaces liquid hot metal, so the casthouse carries costs that primary does not.

| Deduction | Points of LME (EUR/t at 2,155) | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Extra melt loss net of dross recovery: bare extrusion 1.5-2.5%, shredded painted sheet 3-5% | 2.0-3.0 (central 2.5) | ~3% loss × 85% scrap value, less 30-50% recovered from dross [est.] |
| Remelt energy and CO2 against hot metal | 1.4-2.1 (central 1.8) | 0.65-0.8 MWh gas × EUR 30-40 + 0.13 t CO2 × EUR 75 = EUR 30-45/t [est.] |
| Decoating, sampling and LIBS QA, Mg/Si trim | 0.5-1.4 (central 1.0) | EUR 10-30/t [est.] |
| **Total** | **~4-7 (central 5.3)** | |

**Net A3 spread = 15 − 5.3 ≈ 10 points, or EUR 216/t (range 6-13 points, EUR 129-280/t).**

**A3 value.** 15 kt × EUR 216/t = **EUR 3.23M/yr gross**, against EUR 6.46M in the memo: −EUR 3.23M/yr. The range runs from 10 kt × 129 = 1.29M to 30 kt × 280 = 8.40M.

**Effect on NPV.** The CEO's re-run implies about EUR 1.08M of NPV per point of A3 spread ((45.3 − 34.4)/10). On that slope:
- At 10 points, Case A is about EUR 34.4M and committed NPV about EUR 29.9M. This is the CEO's low case, now backed by published prices.
- At the 15-point gross figure, Case A is about EUR 39.9M.
- These are linear approximations, not a model run. The model owner must re-run the script.

**Upside the memo does not count.** The memo values displaced primary at LME flat, but values yield at P1020 duty-paid, EUR 2,431 (+12.8 points). If the casthouse buys external P1020, A3 avoids those purchases at the DP price, and the net spread is about **22 points**. If A3 frees our own metal for sale as ingot, the gain is +12.8 less about 3 points of casting and freight, for a net of about **19 points**. Either case would restore the memo's ~20 points, but on a different logic. Book this only after S&OP confirms the casthouse mass balance (due 6 Nov 2026, Section 6.6). Until then the booked figure stays at **10 points**.

## 4. A1 sweetening, restated with EUR 216/t in place of 431

The method is unchanged: the 5/10/15 cap, split pro rata, with 30/40/50% of yield retained.
- Uncapped sweetening is 2/3.5/5 pp × 216 = **EUR 4.3 / 7.5 / 10.8 per t** (was 8.6/15.1/21.6).
- The yield share of the capped total rises from 36% to 53%.
- The charge-cost component becomes 2.35 / **4.70** / 7.05 (was 3.20/6.39/9.59).
- **A1 net is EUR 3.1 / 6.8 / 11.0 per t**, or EUR 0.94 / **2.05** / 3.31M/yr (was 3.7/7.8/12.3 per t and EUR 2.35M). That is −EUR 0.30M/yr in the base case, worth about −EUR 1.3M of NPV after the haircut and tax [approx.].
- **Gate warning:** the base charge-cost saving of EUR 4.7/t now sits only EUR 0.7/t above T2(a)'s ≥EUR 4/t threshold.
