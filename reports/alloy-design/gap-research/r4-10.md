# T2 condition (c): contracted scrap tonnes and the no-hub valuation

**Bottom line.** I found no public evidence that a mill like ours can sign ~115 kt/yr of lower-rung scrap on firm terms of 3 years or more. The largest disclosed EU deal is a quarter of that. On the tonnes we can realistically contract, Case A with no hub is worth EUR 42–52M, not EUR 80.6M. The proposed 16.5-point internal threshold for the hub falls to 12.5–13.5 points, which is below the hub's own 14-point FID gate. The recommended amendment to the hub FID test does not hold up, and condition (c) needs restating.

## 2. Can about 115 kt/yr be contracted without a hub? [est.]

- **What a deal looks like.** The largest disclosed EU deal is about 25 kt/yr for 3 years, from Europe's largest recycler (7.2 Mt/yr across all metals). The benchmark is roughly 25–40 kt/yr per counterparty, on 1–3 year tenors.
- **115 kt therefore needs 3–5 firm contracts.** We would be competing with Hydro, which is adding about 400–750 kt of post-consumer demand by 2030, plus Novelis and Constellium.
- **What helps.** The lower rungs we need (mixed wrought, taint/tabor) are the grades that are oversupplied or exported. Argus reports Twitch oversupply, and the 1.27 Mt of exports is about 11 times 115 kt.
- **Most of the 100 kt is a re-specification, not new supply.** The A2 tonnes are the casthouse's existing purchased intake (C = 130 kt), bought one grade lower. Existing suppliers can cover much of it by changing the grade they ship.
- **Estimate.** Contractable on firm terms of 3 years or more by 31 Oct 2027: **40–90 kt/yr, central 70 kt** (A2 55 + A3 15) [est.].
- **Cost of reaching 115 kt.** It probably needs a security-of-supply premium of 1–2 points. At 7 or 6 net points instead of 8, Case A with no hub at 100 kt of A2 is EUR 72.6M or 64.6M (model run).
- **Who closes the gap.**
  - Head of Scrap Procurement: 2025–26 invoices by grade (open item 23, due 28 Oct), and the MoU drafts, which should now be **≥3 × 30 kt plus re-specification of existing suppliers**.
  - Market Intelligence: one Fastmarkets MB-AL-0426 print.

## 3. Valuation on contracted tonnes

These figures are a rerun of `alloy_board_model.py` with A2 capped at the contracted tonnes: `ph(y) × min(100, C, T_A2)`. The base case is reproduced exactly: 80.6 at 100 kt of A2 and 42.1 at 40 kt.

| Contracted (A2 + A3 15) | Case A, no hub | IRR | Portfolio P0, no hub (was 76.2) | EV at 50% hub FID, 12 / 14 pts (was 59.0 / 68.6) | Hub internal threshold (was 16.5) | Hub external threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 115 kt (memo) | 80.6 | 55.9% | 76.2 | 59.0 / 68.6 | 16.5 | 14.3 |
| 100 kt | 71.0 | 50.9% | 66.5 | 54.2 / 63.8 | 15.5 | 14.3 |
| 85 kt | 61.4 | 45.9% | 56.9 | 49.4 / 59.0 | 14.5 | 14.3 |
| **70 kt (central est.)** | **51.8** | 40.7% | **47.3** | **44.6 / 54.2** | **13.5** | 14.3 |
| **55 kt (current condition c)** | **42.1** | 35.5% | **37.6** | **39.8 / 49.4** | **12.5** | 14.3 |
| 40 kt | 32.5 | 30.1% | 27.8 | 35.0 / 44.5 | 11.5 | 14.3 |

How the figures are built:
- **Expected value at 50% hub FID** is 0.5 × combined value with the hub internal (37.4 at 12 points, 56.6 at 14 points) + 0.5 × Case A with no hub.
- **Value per tonne.** Each extra kt of A2 adds about (80.6 − 42.1) / 60 = **EUR 0.64M of NPV** after the ramp.
- **Internal threshold** is the spread at which hub value + Case A with the hub built (45.3) equals Case A with no hub at T. The hub's NPV moves about EUR 9.55M per point, so each 15 kt of A2 moves the threshold about 1 point.
- **The 14.3-point external threshold does not move with tonnes.** In that route the casthouse still buys the whole A2 pool on the market. Both sides of the comparison carry the same contracted-tonne limit, so the test reduces to "hub NPV > 0 at −1.5 points".

Tonnage break-evens (total contracted kt):
- **≥60 kt**: the no-hub route beats Case A with the hub built (45.3).
- **≥78 kt**: it beats the combined value with the hub at the FID gate of 14 points (56.6).
- **≥92 kt**: it beats the combined value at the 15-point ladder central (66.1).

## 4. Restated condition (c) and the hub test

1. **The alternative-source route releases T2 only on ≥3-year contracts for ≥60 kt/yr** of grade-certified mixed-wrought or taint/tabor scrap plus A3 post-consumer, priced as % of LME.
   - Below 60 kt, the no-hub route is worth less than Case A with the hub built.
   - The 55 kt sizing is kept only for the hub-FID route, where 40 kt + 15 kt is still bought on the market.
2. **Case A with no hub is booked on contracted tonnes:** NPV = 42.1 + 0.64 × (T − 55) for T between 55 and 115 kt. The central figure is EUR 51.8M, not 80.6M. EUR 80.6M is reached only at 115 kt contracted at 8 net points.
3. **The 16.5-point internal threshold for the hub is withdrawn.** On central tonnes it is 13.5 points (range 12.5–15.5 for 55–100 kt), which is below the existing 14-point FID gate.
   - The hub's own 14-point gate is therefore sufficient.
   - Tighten it only if procurement contracts ≥85 kt before M12.
   - The external-offtake threshold stays at 14.3 points.
