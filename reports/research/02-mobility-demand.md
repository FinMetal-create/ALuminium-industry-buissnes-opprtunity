## EVs, Automotive Lightweighting & Gigacasting

### 1. Executive summary

Automotive is the largest growth market for European aluminium. Electrification adds more than 100 kg of aluminium per car. The growth is less certain than in 2023 for three reasons:
- **The EU has loosened its targets.** The December 2025 Automotive Package proposes replacing the 2035 "100% CO2 cut" with a 90% cut plus credits.
- **European car output is flat.** It is about 17 Mu a year.
- **Chinese competition is rising fast.** Chinese OEMs are taking market share and starting to build cars inside the EU.

Three structural changes favour an integrated EU aluminium company with a smelter, casthouse, extrusion, rolling and recycling:
1. **Large castings need new alloys.** Megacasting and gigacasting move value toward high-volume, non-heat-treatable structural die-casting alloys that tolerate high recycled content.
2. **Regulation makes scrap valuable.** Recycled-content rules (ELV Regulation) and scrap-export restrictions turn automotive scrap into a strategic asset.
3. **"Low-carbon" and "Made in Europe" may become rules.** Both criteria are being written into EU car rules (CO2 credits, Industrial Accelerator Act) and could become a real price premium, not just marketing.

The best opportunities are in alloy and scrap loops, not in competing with Tier-1s on finished parts.

### 2. Demand fundamentals: kg per vehicle

- **Average European car.** Aluminium content rose from 174 kg (2019) to 205 kg (2022). The projection is 237 kg in 2026 and 256 kg in 2030 (Ducker Carlisle for European Aluminium, 2023; [Aluminium Today](https://aluminiumtoday.com/news/aluminium-usage-in-cars-surges-as-automotive-industry-shifts-towards-electrification), [AL Circle](https://www.alcircle.com/news/aluminium-usage-in-cars-spikes-as-electrification-takes-off-in-the-automotive-sector-93763)). I found no 2026 edition of that study.
- **BEV vs ICE.** A European-built BEV held 283 kg of aluminium on average in 2022, against 169 kg for an ICE-only car. BEV content is projected at about 310 kg in 2026. The BEV premium of about 115 kg comes mostly from three things:
  - the battery enclosure (up to about 100 kg in premium/SUV packs, per Hydro via [CRU](https://www.crugroup.com/en/communities/thought-leadership/2019/aluminium-extrusion-firms-reinforce-focus-on-high-value-added-products/));
  - crash management to protect the pack;
  - e-drive housings for motor, inverter, reducer and heat pump.
- **Sheet-based enclosures use less metal.** Novelis's sheet-based enclosure claims a frame mass below 1.0 kg per kWh ([Novelis](https://novelis.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Novelis-Battery-Enclosure-Factsheet.pdf)). That means about 60–75 kg for a mid-size pack. Extrusion-intensive designs are heavier. Cell-to-body/cell-to-chassis designs (Tesla structural pack, BYD CTB) blur the line between enclosure and body-in-white. This is a downside risk for dedicated enclosure tonnage.
- **Tonnage (secondary reporting).** One analysis sizes European automotive aluminium demand at about 4.2 Mt a year by 2030, against about 2.5 Mt in 2022. It assumes 16.5 Mu of production and a 60% BEV share. About 42% of automotive aluminium is currently recycled ([AL Circle](https://www.alcircle.com/news/primary-vs-recycled-al-which-will-dominate-as-europes-automotive-sector-heads-toward-yearly-4-2mt-of-aluminium-demand-by-2030-119786)). Treat the 60% BEV share as optimistic.
- **Our own cross-check (estimate).** S&P Global puts European light-vehicle production at 17.04 Mu in 2025, 16.86 Mu in 2026 and 17.42 Mu in 2030 ([S&P LVP summary](https://cdn.ihsmarkit.com/www/pdf/0426/Global-Production-Summary_LVP.pdf)). At 256 kg per vehicle, 2030 aluminium content is about **4.4–4.5 Mt**. Our 2035 scenario assumes a 65–75% BEV/EREV mix at about 320 kg and the rest at about 190 kg. That gives **4.8–5.0 Mt** of content, or roughly **+0.8–1.0 Mt/yr** of new automotive demand in Europe between 2026 and 2035. The weaker 2035 rule is the main downside: ICCT warns the 2030 BEV share could be 17 points lower (44% vs 61%) ([ICCT](https://theicct.org/publication/european-commissions-risks-and-opportunities-of-the-proposed-co₂-standards-dec25)).

### 3. EU EV market and the China factor

- **EV market.** The EU BEV share was 20.0% in January–May 2026 (950,521 units, +35.7% y/y), up from 15.3% a year earlier ([ACEA via NewMobility](https://newmobility.news/en/2026/06/23/acea-eu-car-market-grows-4-in-may-ev-shift-is-reshaping-industry/)). Across Europe, EVs including PHEVs reached a 28% share in 2025 ([IEA GEVO 2026](https://iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/trends-in-electric-cars)).
- **Policy.** The December 2025 proposal sets the 2035 target at -90% CO2. The remaining 10% can be offset by credits, including for **low-carbon steel "made in the Union"** ([ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/eu-to-scrap-requirements-for-100-emissions-reduction-from-new-cars-by-2035/), [Akin Gump](https://www.akingump.com/en/insights/alerts/steering-toward-2035-eus-automotive-sector-gets-a-package)). European Aluminium (February 2026) and a joint European Aluminium–ACEA statement (May 2026) call for:
  - low-carbon aluminium to be added to the credit system;
  - the credits to start as soon as the review is adopted, not in 2035;
  - an ESPR low-carbon aluminium definition by end-2027 ([European Aluminium](https://european-aluminium.eu/news_events/european-aluminium-calls-for-the-inclusion-of-low-carbon-aluminium-in-the-2035-automotive-credit-system/), [joint statement](https://european-aluminium.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/26-05-18-Joint-statement-European-Aluminium_ACEA_CO2-Rules.pdf)).

  **This is a potential direct monetisation route for our low-CO2 metal.**
- **Chinese competition.**
  - **Market share.** Chinese brands reached 10.7% (May) and 10.9% (June 2026) of the European market (Dataforce, EU+UK+EFTA). In the EU alone, ACEA puts their share at about 6% for January–April ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/business/2026/05/27/chinese-carmakers-double-eu-market-share-as-evs-drive-sales-growth)).
  - **Trade measures.** Countervailing duties of up to 35.3% on top of the 10% import duty can now be replaced by minimum-price and volume-cap undertakings (Commission guidance, 12 January 2026) ([Automotive World](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news/china-eu-reach-framework-for-ev-price-minimums/)).
  - **Local production.** It is starting:
    - **BYD Szeged (Hungary):** start in Q4 2026, 150k units rising to 300k, about €4bn investment. Turkey is on hold.
    - **Chery/Ebro (Barcelona):** about 130k units capacity.
    - **XPeng and GAC:** assembly at Magna Steyr Graz ([MarketScreener/Reuters](https://uk.marketscreener.com/news/byd-hungary-plant-to-start-production-in-late-2026-executive-says-ce7f5cdadc8af624), [Gasgoo](https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/news/beyond-exports-chinese-automakers-build-a-production-footprint-in-europe-2089241385969664000)).
  - **What this means for us.** Chinese imports carry Chinese aluminium. EU-built Chinese cars are new local customers who need local-content credentials.
- **Incumbent weakness.** Constellium's FY2025 10-K says automotive demand "remained weak in Europe". Its Automotive Structures & Industry segment revenue was about USD 1.58bn, with flat shipments ([SEC 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1563411/000156341126000057/cstm-20251231.htm)). EU OEM volume is not a growth engine on its own. Growth comes from more content per car, low-carbon premiums and new customers.

### 4. Gigacasting / megacasting: adoption and alloy implications

- **Tesla.** Tesla pioneered the approach (Model Y front and rear castings). The Cybercab "unboxed" line at Giga Texas combines gigacasting with structural battery integration. First units came off the line in February 2026 ([InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/news/778232/tesla-cybercab-production-april-2026/)).
- **Volvo.** The EX60 (2026) is Volvo's first megacast car, with a single-piece rear underbody. It is made in Torslanda on Bühler Carat 840 cells (8,400 t) ([Euroguss](https://www.euroguss.de/en/euroguss-365/2026/02/megacasting-at-volvo), [Foundry Mag](https://www.foundrymag.com/molds-cores/article/21242517/volvo-orders-two-megacasting-machines-buhler-ag)).
- **VW Group.** VW has reportedly ordered **16 Bühler 9,000 t machines** (reported split: 8 VW, 4 Audi, 4 Škoda) for front and rear underbodies on its next-generation EV platform. VW has not confirmed this. Its cast battery frames already replace 123 parts, with about -10% weight and -15% cost ([Castings SA](https://castingssa.com/volkswagen-group-orders-16-of-9-000-ton-gigacasting-machines-from-buhler/)).
- **Toyota.** Aisin plans about USD 3.4bn of giga-casting investment, and there is a 2×9,000 t line in Shanghai ([Foundry Planet](https://www.foundry-planet.com/d/lexus-relies-on-9000-ton-gigacasting-in-shanghai/)).
- **Hyundai.** Its "hypercasting" has reportedly slipped by about two years, to around 2028 ([Light Metal Age](https://www.lightmetalage.com/news/industry-news/automotive/giga-castings-in-the-automotive-industry-an-updated-review/)).
- **China leads in scale:**
  - Xiaomi SU7 uses 2×9,100 t presses.
  - Dongfeng has a 16,000 t LK line, with mass production from June 2026.
  - Wencan has two 12,000 t presses ([Gigacasting Newsletter](https://lucagreco.beehiiv.com/p/weekly-gigacasting-news-26-d70e511341e34c56)).
  - By 2030, about 70% of large structural castings (>3,000 t machines) are expected to be giga-castings. Outside China they are mostly made in-house by OEMs, with "very few Tier 1s" ([Light Metal Age](https://www.lightmetalage.com/news/industry-news/automotive/giga-castings-in-the-automotive-industry-an-updated-review/)).
  - Bühler has passed 50 megacasting orders ([Bühler](https://www.buhlergroup.com/global/vi/stories/buhler-celebrates-50-megacasting-solutions.html)).
- **Alloys.** Gigacastings must reach crash ductility without a heat-treatment furnace, which would distort a 2 m part. That drives demand for **non-heat-treatable (NHT) HPDC alloys**, for example Alcoa C611 EZCast, which reaches its properties after paint-bake ([Foundry Planet](https://foundry-planet.com/d/alcoa-recognized-for-alloyinnovation-as-megacasting-trend-in-auto-industry-grows)). Chalmers (2025) found alloys with up to **90% secondary content** matched primary-based alloys mechanically, provided chemistry (especially Fe) is controlled ([Chalmers](https://odr.chalmers.se/items/6d25397e-a96e-4330-9a08-af108cab42df)). The bottleneck is clean, well-sorted scrap and tight melt chemistry, which favours integrated producers with primary "sweetening" capacity and casthouse know-how.
- **Competitor moves:**
  - Hydro–Nemak LOI (2025): foundry alloys below **3.0 kg CO2e/kg**, from REDUXA at below 4.0. Alumetal (Poland, owned by Hydro) supplies the recycled share ([Hydro](https://integration.hydro.com/en/global/media/news/2025/hydro-and-nemak-sign-loi-to-decarbonize-automotive-industry/)).
  - Rio Tinto–Comptech: rheocasting alloys for giga-castings in qualification with EU OEMs ([Rio Tinto](https://www.riotinto.com/en/news/releases/2021/rio-tinto-and-comptech-partner-to-deliver-next-generation-of-electric-vehicle-and-5g-giga-casting-alloys)).
- **Gigacasting alloy demand in Europe (our estimate).** A typical underbody megacasting weighs about 30–80 kg. Assume:
  - 16 VW machines at about 120–160k shots a year each, about 50 kg per shot;
  - plus Volvo, Tesla Berlin and Chinese transplants;
  - plus 15–30% runner and overflow remelt.

  That gives about **150–300 kt/yr of structural HPDC alloy in Europe by 2030–2032**, and possibly 400 kt+ by 2035 if a second wave of OEMs adopts. This is a new, specified, premium market of a size that one or two dedicated casthouse lines can address.

### 5. Recycling and regulation: the scrap loop

- **ELV Regulation.** It took effect in August 2026 and covers cars, vans, trucks, buses and motorcycles. The Commission must set **minimum recycled-content requirements for steel and aluminium, applying by 14 August 2033 at the latest**. The plastics targets are 15% rising to 25%, with a share required to come from end-of-life vehicles (closed loop). Only roadworthy vehicles may be exported from mid-2031 ([Just Auto](https://just-auto.com/news/eu-end-of-life-vehicle-regulation-takes-effect/), [Circular Online](https://www.circularonline.co.uk/news/eu-agrees-tougher-end-of-life-vehicles-rules-to-boost-recycled-content-and-cut-material-losses/)). Keeping old cars in the EU means more post-consumer aluminium stays in the EU.
- **Scrap exports.** The EU exported about **1.26–1.27 Mt of aluminium scrap** in 2024–2025, and exports kept rising in 2026. The Commission dropped the proposed export duty for a delegated act under the Waste Shipment Regulation. It would restrict exports to non-OECD countries from about 2027, with exemptions for free-trade partners ([Mining Weekly/Reuters](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/eu-aluminium-scrap-export-curbs-delayed-until-september-group-and-sources-say-2026-06-26), [Hellenic Shipping News](https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/european-aluminium-irked-by-delay-replacement-of-scrap-export-duty/)).
- **Existing closed loops.** Closed loops with OEMs are proven for sheet:
  - Audi Aluminium Closed Loop: Neckarsulm, Ingolstadt, Győr, VW Bratislava; more than 725 kt CO2 avoided cumulatively.
  - Novelis with Smart Press Shop, BMW, Volvo and JLR ([VW Group](https://www.volkswagengroup.it/en/vision/aluminum-closed-loop-a-virtuous-cycle-to-reduce-waste-and-emissions), [Novelis](https://novelis.com/news/closed-loop-recycling-partnership-novelis-and-smart-press-shop/)).

  The open space is closed loops for **casting returns and post-consumer ELV scrap**, sorted by alloy family (wrought vs cast) with sensor sorting such as LIBS and XRT.

### 6. Commercial vehicles, buses, rail

- **Uptake.** In H1 2026, electrically-chargeable trucks reached a 4.8% EU share (+47.7%) and e-buses 27.7% (+56.8%) ([ACEA](https://www.acea.auto/cv-registrations/new-commercial-vehicle-registrations-vans-1-9-trucks-9-8-buses-22-7-in-h1-2026/)). Zero-emission heavy trucks above 12 t were only 1.9% in 2025 ([ICCT](https://theicct.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/ID-565-–-EU-HDV-Q1–Q4-2025_market-spotlight_final-1.pdf)).
- **Aluminium uses.** E-trucks need large aluminium battery boxes. Freightliner's eCascadia uses one, and aluminium is about 40% lighter than steel ([SAE](https://saemobilus.sae.org/content/21AUTP04_08/)). Weight-sensitive payload also favours aluminium wheels and wheel-ends: about 70% are aluminium in the US, while Europe and China mostly still use iron ([Motor India](https://www.motorindiaonline.in/conmet-bets-on-lighter-wheel-ends-as-heavier-ev-trucks-arrive-in-europe/)).
- **E-buses.** They are a niche but growing market for large-profile, aluminium-intensive bodies. Our estimate is 1–2.5 t of aluminium per 12 m e-bus with an aluminium body.
- **Rail.** Large hollow extrusions for high-speed and metro car bodies are a stable, specialised outlet. They fit well with any large-press capability we have.

### 8. So what for our company

1. **Make structural HPDC alloy our main automotive product.** Target a high-recycled-content (≥70%), NHT, low-CO2 (<3–4 kg CO2e/kg) alloy, qualified with VW, Volvo, Tesla Berlin and the Chinese transplants. Primary metal from our smelter is the "sweetener" that keeps Fe and trace elements in check, and gives us a cost and chemistry edge over pure remelters.
2. **Lock in scrap before regulation drives up its value.** Sign multi-year take-back contracts covering:
   - gigacasting returns, which are 15–30% of shot weight;
   - press-shop offcuts;
   - ELV shred via partnerships with shredders.

   Make the contracts ELV-2033 compliant, with ASI chain-of-custody and digital product passports.
3. **Get our CO2 data ready to sell as credits.** If aluminium enters the 2035 credit system or the Industrial Accelerator Act "Made in Europe" rules, the low-carbon premium could become regulated value. Back European Aluminium's lobbying and have verified product footprints ready.
4. **Sell to Chinese transplants rather than resist them.** BYD Szeged, Chery Barcelona and Magna-built XPeng/GAC cars all need EU-sourced, low-carbon content to meet local-content rules and price undertakings. Offer alloy, extrusions and scrap take-back in one package.
5. **Be selective on battery enclosures.** Volumes are real, but cell-to-body designs, sheet/roll-formed alternatives and Tier-1 margins are risks. Partner with a Tier-1 rather than build full assembly lines.
6. **Use commercial vehicles to hedge.** E-truck battery boxes, aluminium wheel-ends and e-bus/rail extrusions are smaller markets but stickier, and less exposed to Chinese car competition.
